# broadleaf_tree — candidates and choice

- Winner: **broadleaf_tree_c** (718 tris, 7 meshes, measured 5.32 x 6.16 x 4.45 m). Trunk and five limbs
  (0.28 m and 0.10 m dia, 7 sides, spreading 20..45 degrees) are one hand-built loft; six hash-lumped
  icosahedra (detail 1, radii 0.9..1.3 m) sit one per limb end with the tips buried 0.3 m, plus one hanging
  low off limb 1 so the crown runs 2.4..6.0 m. Every blob is on a limb, the limbs show through the gaps from
  the front, back and three-quarter views, and the lumps stop the balls reading as balloons; it is the most
  open and zelkova-like of the three, and it shares the maple winner's blob language. ONE `foliage` material,
  maple gold, ready to be retinted per instance.
- broadleaf_tree_a (728 tris, plain icosahedra on cylinder limbs) is a close second, but its small
  gap-filler blob has no limb reaching it and hangs visibly detached below the crown in the right and back
  views, which reads as a bug rather than an open crown.
- broadleaf_tree_b (826 tris, 7-sided lathes throughout) is heaviest, its blobs overlap into a closed
  pumpkin-textured dome so the limbs vanish, and it came in at 4.25 m wide (18 % under, still inside the
  0.25 tolerance). Its profiles were written bottom -> top from the start (the lathe winding trap), so it
  rendered right side out first time.
- After the render: nothing changed. All three passed the gate first time (base at 0, centred, no blank side).
  C's depth is 4.45 m against the 5.0 m header (11 % under): the limbs on the z axis are the shorter ones.
- Final: 718 triangles, inside the 350–900 band.
