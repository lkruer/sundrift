# chevron_sign — notes

- Winner: **B** (profile route: board as two extruded rounded rectangles, gold face over galvanised back; border as an extruded frame Shape with a hole; each chevron an extruded ">" Shape; post a lathe with foot flare and domed top; back stiffeners extruded C-channels). 572 triangles, measured 0.92 x 1.8 x 0.132 m.
- Why B: the extruded ">" gives the sharpest mitred chevron tips of the three, the rounded board corners read as a real sign blank, and the C-channel rails give the back two lines each so the -Z face is clearly a sign back. A's two-bar chevrons leave a small flat at each tip and a slight overshoot at the outer ends; C's stacked-plate border reads as a margin rather than a frame.
- A (312 tris) and C (346 tris, hand-built prism chevrons) both pass the verifier and read as the sign from every view; nothing was broken in either, B is simply crisper.
- Nothing changed after the render: all three passed first time. The chevrons point +X (to the right in the front view), the face is on +Z, the post is behind the board.
- Depth: post 0.06 behind a 0.04 board plus 0.01 chevrons is 0.11; the clamp rings and foot flare at 0.076 dia bring the measured depth to 0.132 against the brief's 0.12, inside tolerance.
- Materials: post, back plate and rails `metal` galvanised; face maple gold; chevrons, border, clamps chrome dark. No text or glyphs, signage is carried by colour and shape only.
