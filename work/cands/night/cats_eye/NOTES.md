# cats_eye — candidate notes

- Winner: **cats_eye_c.js** (chamfered octagonal wedge), shipped as `game/assets/cats_eye.js`. Final: 208 triangles, 8 meshes, measured 0.16 x 0.045 x 0.14 m (the rubber base plate is the footprint; the white housing is 0.14 x 0.12 as briefed), base at y = 0, centred, all four sides judged and none blank.
- Why C: the brief asks for a shallow wedge with rounded ends, and an 8-sided drum under an 8-sided frustum, flat shaded, is exactly a box with chamfers: crisp facets that a cel band and an ink outline will pick up, one clean silhouette from every side, and the cheapest of the three for something instanced hundreds of times. The tail-red and lane-white lenses sit in chrome-dark surrounds on the two long facets, the rubber torus gasket gives the sides their own edges.
- Changed after seeing the renders: the octagon was first stretched by a plain X scale, which left its front facet 0.054 m long under a 0.074 m surround, so the surround corners floated in the air at both ends. It is now stretched by pushing every vertex outward on X by a constant (a chamfered rectangle, not a squashed oval) and the radii are set from the octagon's apothem, so the housing measures 0.14 x 0.12 and the plate 0.16 x 0.14 rather than 0.149 x 0.129. Surround narrowed to 0.07 to match the facet.
- A (primitives, 236 tris): a stadium puck from a box and two cylinders with a stepped upper puck. Clean at the gate, but the plan is nearly a circle, the step reads as a lid, and the rubber grip pads on the ends read as blocks glued on. Thrown away.
- B (profiles, 316 tris): a 10-segment lathe dome scaled into an oval in a lathe gasket, extruded rounded-rectangle lenses and plate. Smooth and tidy, but it reads as a mushroom rather than a road stud, the tilted lens surrounds poke through the dome at their top corners from the side views, and it is over the 300 ceiling. Thrown away.
- Deliberate deviations: the housing uses flatShading (the style lock reserves flat shading for foliage and rock) because a smooth-shaded 8-sided drum reads as a lumpy cylinder and the brief wants a chamfered box; the plate, lenses and gasket are thinner than the lock's 0.04 m minimum because the brief specifies them at 0.01 to 0.02 m for a 0.045 m tall object. The lane-white lens emits at 0.35 as briefed (lock: nothing but lamp lenses and tail-lights emit).
