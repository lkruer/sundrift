# conbini — candidate notes

- Winner: **conbini_c.js** (the shop as a lit box you look into), shipped as `game/assets/conbini.js`. Final: 2,748 triangles, 186 meshes, measured 9.65 x 4.76 x 6.8 m (the 9 x 4.2 x 6 box plus the exhaust hood, the roof plant and the bollard pavement), base at y = 0, centred, all four sides judged and none blank (front 0.199, back 0.097, right 0.086, left 0.076 edge density).
- Why C: it is the one that reads as a conbini at night rather than a concrete box with a red band. The vermilion fascia is a projecting canopy with three lamp-warm down-lights under it and the lane-white stripe on its face; the glazing has a galvanised mid-rail with lane-white spandrel panels below it, so the front carries a bright band at car height with the interior visible above it; one sliding-door leaf is slid open behind the other so the doorway reads as open; the front wall is a Shape with a hole (a real opening, no stacked bands); the emissive 8 x 5 ceiling panel sits in a galvanised tile grid and three pendant lamp lenses hang at 2.42 m so warm light shows through the glass from any camera height (the ceiling panel at 2.875 m is only visible from a low chase camera); the gondolas have galvanised end caps and goods on them; the roof carries two AC units on a thin dark frame and an access hatch for a stepped skyline; a dark plinth, floor and coping give the style lock's dark lines.
- Changed after seeing the renders: (1) depth was 7.32 m (22 percent over) and height 4.97 m, so the pavement slab, bollards, step and canopy were pulled in and the AC units lowered to 0.4 m on a 0.06 m frame, giving 6.8 x 4.76; (2) the pendant lamps were added because at the verifier's 25-degree camera the interior read as a dark mirror; (3) after those two changes the back fell to 0.349 of the front's edge density (limit 0.35), so it gained what a real back wall has: a galvanised roof-access ladder, a small framed staff window and a meter cabinet, and the borderline left side gained a louvre vent.
- A (primitives, 1,600 tris): every part a box, cylinder or torus. Complete and clean, but the fascia is a flat band, the doors are both shut, the interior is dark boxes behind dark glass. Thrown away.
- B (profiles, 2,384 tris): C-plan extruded shell, extruded coping frame, sloped lightbox fascia section, T-section mullions, comb-profile shelves with real shelf planes, section-swept counter and steps, lathe bollards with domed tops. The comb shelves and domed bollards are the best individual parts of the three, and its light concrete roof under a dark coping is handsome from above, but a chase camera never sees the roof and the front reads the same as A. Thrown away.
- Deliberate deviations to flag: the bollards, pavement slab and kerb sit in front of the 6 m box (depth 6.8, 13 percent over), the exhaust hood and coping make the width 9.65 (7 percent), the roof plant makes the height 4.76 (13 percent); all inside the 0.25 tolerance. Tint glass is transparent at opacity 0.5 as briefed (the loader's transparent-pass cost applies: one glass material, single sided). The ceiling panel emits at 1.6 as briefed; the pendant and canopy lenses at the lock's 1.2. The window on the left side, the ladder, the staff window, the cabinet and the louvre are additions to "plain concrete" sides so no face is blank; the material for concrete is named `plaster` as for the tunnel portal.
- Unresolved: with the game's own lighting the interior glow depends on bloom over the emissive panel and pendants; if it reads too dark, the floor slab (chrome dark by brief) is the first thing to lighten.
