# hero_coupe — candidate notes (receipts)

- Winner: **hero_coupe_c.js** (hand-built loft), shipped as `game/assets/hero_coupe.js`. Final: 10,352 triangles, 174 meshes, measured 1.938 x 1.289 x 4.491 m (width includes the mirrors; body is 1.72), base at y = 0, centred, all four sides within 0.127–0.164 edge density so no side is blank. Joints declared: hubFL, hubFR (steer pivots, 4°/3° camber baked into rotation.z), wheelFL/FR/RL/RR (spin), everything static under 'body'.
- Why C: it is the only candidate whose paint reads as one sculpted surface. Ten 12-point sections skinned with crease-aware smoothed normals give a crowned bonnet and roof, a belt crease, and the glasshouse as a second material group inside the same skin, so the windscreen, side glass and rear glass are continuous with the body and the seats, cage bar and helmeted driver show through. Flares are a 4-point loop swept around each arch (flat-topped box flare, real opening), bumpers and lamp band are plan-curved lofts, tyres/rims/discs/helmet are revolved loops. Judged on the side and three-quarter renders, it is the one that looks like a low 90s coupe rather than an assembly.
- A (primitives, 13,228 tris): complete and clean at the gate, but the seven-box polygonal flares read as blocks glued to the flanks, the roof panel reads as a lid sitting on the glass box, and the flat-sided door boxes give no shoulder.
